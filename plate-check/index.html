How the score works

School-zone speed or red light: 10 points. Bike lane, bus lane, hydrant, or double parking: 6. Other parking: 2. Meters, street cleaning, or stickers: 1. Moving summons: 5. Dismissed or not-guilty tickets don’t count. Score = 100 × (1 − e^(−points/35)).
