How the score works

Recent school-zone speed or red-light records: 10 points each. Bike lane, bus lane, hydrant, or double parking: 6. Other parking: 2. Meters, street cleaning, or stickers: 1. Moving summonses: 5. Known dismissed or not-guilty parking rows do not score. Points become a 0–100 scale using 100 × (1 − e^(−points/35)). No records means “no signal,” not a clean driving record.

Public data can be incomplete or corrected, and plates can move between vehicles. This is commentary on a plate’s records, not a claim about its current driver. NYC data terms ↗
